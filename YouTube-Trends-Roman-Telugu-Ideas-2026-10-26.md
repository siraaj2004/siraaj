## Verdict — Which genre is booming?

### India — BOOMING #1: Thriller / True Crime / Mystery + Vertical Micro-Drama
- Shorts drive 60%+ of discovery in India and link to long-form; vernacular content is the growth engine.
- India faceless winners in 2026: devotional & mythology, legal awareness, true crime, stock-market explainers, motivational stories, Tier-2 local news.
- Telugu Shorts right now are full of suspense-thriller short films, locked-room mysteries, CID-style investigation and horror-mystery series.
- Malayalam-thriller formula that travels: believable characters, realistic police work, layered clues, moral conflict, logical twist — not loud villains.

### World — BOOMING
- Long-form views +76% YoY, Shorts views +127% YoY, Shorts feed = 61% of all views (Metricool 2026, 799,718 videos).
- Vertical micro-drama (60-90 sec episodes): ~8x growth vs 2023. AI-narrated revenge/betrayal long-form: ~5x. K/C-drama recaps: ~3x.
- Breakout rate: Personal finance 16.7%, True crime / unsolved mysteries 12.5%, Cooking 11.7%, AI tools 10.0%.
- Fastest growing overall: AI Tools & Tutorials ~+340%, No-Code & Automation +220%, Personal Finance Gen Z +180%.

### Strategy for a Telugu channel
Shorts = hook + cliffhanger + discovery. Long-form = trust + watch-time + money.
Post 5 Shorts/month minimum to lift long-form; best long-form cadence 2-4/week in reports, but for a thriller channel 1 strong long-form + 5-7 Shorts/week is realistic.
Every Short must end with one unanswered question. Every long-form must plant 3 clues in the first 3 minutes and pay off only one at the end.

## High-CTR rules (Malayalam-thriller style)
1. Hook in first 5 seconds: a phone call, a missing person, a CCTV frame — not an introduction.
2. One mystery, one moral dilemma, one twist. No extra subplots.
3. Thumbnail text = 3-4 words, one face + one clue object (key, phone, CCTV, diary).
4. Title = curiosity gap, not spoiler. Example pattern: "Aa Call Tarvata Athanu Kanipinchaledu!"
5. End with a question for comments: "Meeru aithe em chestaru? Comment lo cheppandi."

## LONG-FORM IDEAS — Roman Telugu (12-25 min each)

### L1. Guntur Lo Missing Auto Driver: Last Call 11:47 PM
Hook: Auto driver Raju last call lo "Nenu safe ga ledu" ani cheppi phone cut chestadu.
Story: Police ki dorikina CCTV lo auto undi, driver ledu. Passenger evaru? Diary lo oka address.
Twist: Passenger ye kaadu — driver ye oka purani case ki witness.
CTR: Thumbnail "11:47 PM | LAST CALL" + empty auto photo.
Why it works: True crime + local Guntur + realistic police work.

### L2. Drishyam Style: Oka Family, Oka Night, Oka Lie
Hook: Oka middle-class father, oka accident, aa tarvata family motham oka abaddham cheptundi.
Story: Prathi member version lo oka chinna difference. Police officer adi pattukuntadu.
Twist: Nijam ga tappu chesindi father kaadu — koduku ni kapadataniki father blame tesukuntadu.
CTR: "Family Antha Okate Lie Cheptondi — Enduku?"
Malayalam feel: Family emotion + moral dilemma, no heroism, only choices.

### L3. Room No. 13: Lock Chesina Room Lo Hatya Ela Jarigindi?
Hook: Bayata nundi lock chesina room lo oka vyakti chanipoyadu. Killer ela bayataki velladu?
Story: 5 suspects, prathi okkariki oka motive, oka secret. Ventilation, duplicate key, old servant clue.
Twist: Killer room lo ne unnadu — police uniform lo.
CTR: "Locked Room Murder | Killer Evaru?"

### L4. Phone Lo Unna 3 Photos: Oka Ammayi Missing Case

Story: Photos lo background lo kanipinche oka shop board, oka bus number, oka shadow.
Twist: Ammayi ni kidnap cheyaledu — thane oka scam nundi tappinchukotaniki hide ayyindi.
CTR: "3 Photos Matrame Migilayi — Aa Ammayi Ekkada?"

### L5. Bank Scam: Oka Clerk, 47 Crores, Oka Diary

### L6. Village Lo Jarigina Oka Nyayam: Sarpanch Hatya
