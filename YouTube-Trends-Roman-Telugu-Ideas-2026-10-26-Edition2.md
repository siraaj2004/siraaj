## Verdict: which genre is booming?

**Suspense Thriller / True-Crime Mystery is No. 1 in India and No. 1 worldwide.**
Horror-Mystery is No. 2 right now for Halloween week (31 Oct).
The winning Telugu play: Shorts create the curiosity, long-form pays it off.

## India trends — long-form vs Shorts

- Shorts: 60%+ of discovery, and Shorts now link onward to long-form. About 73% of India's internet users consume content in regional languages. Telugu suspense Shorts are already strong.
- Long-form: connected TV rising fast (six-figure-earning channels +45% in a year). Faceless winners include true crime, devotional/mythology, legal awareness and Tier-2 local stories.

## World trends — long-form vs Shorts

- Long-form views +76% YoY (Metricool 2026 study, 799,718 videos). True crime favours 20–45 min.
- Shorts views +127% YoY, 61% of views, 70B+ daily views. Shorts are the discovery layer; long-form is the destination.

## Malayalam-thriller formula + high-CTR system

Ordinary person, realistic investigation, 3 clues in the first 3 minutes, one moral dilemma, a logical twist (not a shock twist), quiet ending. Titles = curiosity gap, thumbnails = 3–4 words max + one clue object, hook in the first 5 seconds, every Short ends with one unanswered question.
